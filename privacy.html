CUSTOMER DATA

Privacy.

What SafiSolutions collects, why it is collected, and how it is used.

Effective September 18, 2026

Short version: SafiSolutions collects only the information needed to process purchases, answer inquiries, provide downloads, and support customers. SafiSolutions does not sell customer data.

1. Information you provide

When you contact SafiSolutions, request support, or ask for an update, you may provide your name, email address, business or project name, phone number, product, version, order reference, and the contents of your message.

2. Purchase information

Checkout is handled by Stripe. Stripe processes payment-card information. SafiSolutions receives order information such as your email address, purchased product or bundle, payment status, Stripe order/session identifiers, and related transaction details needed for fulfillment, support, refunds, and accounting. SafiSolutions does not receive your full card number.

3. Forms and service providers

Website inquiry and support forms are delivered through FormSubmit. Stripe handles checkout. Hosting and security providers may process ordinary technical logs. When secure downloads are activated, Cloudflare may process requests needed to verify and deliver private product files. These providers operate under their own privacy terms.

4. How information is used

  • Process and verify purchases.
  • Deliver purchased software.
  • Answer questions and support requests.
  • Confirm update eligibility.
  • Prevent fraud or abuse.
  • Maintain business and tax records where required.
  • Improve SafiSolutions products and customer experience.

5. Sharing

SafiSolutions does not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers only when needed to operate checkout, forms, hosting, downloads, email, security, accounting, or legal compliance.

6. Retention

Order records may be kept as long as reasonably needed for ownership verification, support, refunds, accounting, fraud prevention, and legal obligations. General inquiries may be deleted when they are no longer useful.

7. Cookies and tracking

SafiSolutions does not intentionally run an advertising-tracking system on this website. Third-party services used for checkout, forms, hosting, or security may use necessary cookies or similar technology when you interact with them.

8. Your choices

You may contact SafiSolutions to ask what personal information is associated with your inquiry or purchase, request a correction, or request deletion when retention is not required for legitimate business or legal reasons.

9. Contact

Privacy requests can be sent to safihelal@gmail.com.
