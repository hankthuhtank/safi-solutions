# TradeSchool V11 — Visual honesty pass

## Problems fixed
1. **Unit-level “Field References” strips removed.** They repeated the same photo three times (e.g. multimeter ×3) and added noise above every unit.
2. **Bad motor nameplate crop removed.** The crop was small, washed out, and showed almost nothing readable. The nameplate concept now uses the full motor photo with a caption that tells the learner to read the plate on the frame.
3. **Aggressive over-mapping rolled back.** Voltage, current, RMS, and other abstract measurement concepts no longer inherit a multimeter photo just to “have an image.” Images only appear on concepts where the photo is the actual equipment being taught.
4. **Duplicate suppression kept** inside a unit: if two concepts share the same file, the image renders once.

## Rules now in force
- One meaningful image per concept, or none.
- No unit-level visual galleries.
- No decorative or repeated images.
- Prefer a clear full equipment shot over a useless detail crop.

## Coverage
~42 concepts keep a visual — fewer than V10’s 113, but every remaining one is intentional.
