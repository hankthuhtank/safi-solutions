DIGITAL PRODUCT POLICY

Refunds.

A practical refund policy for one-time downloadable SafiSolutions software.

Effective September 18, 2026

Goal: If you genuinely bought the wrong thing, were charged twice, or the software will not work on a supported system after reasonable troubleshooting, reach out. The point is to solve the problem, not trap someone in a bad purchase.

1. Digital-product purchases

SafiSolutions products are downloadable software delivered after payment. Because a digital product can be copied after delivery, refunds are not automatic simply because the installer has been downloaded.

2. When to contact SafiSolutions

If there is a purchase problem, contact safihelal@gmail.com within 14 days of purchase when practical. Include the checkout email, product name, and a short explanation.

3. Situations normally eligible for correction

  • Duplicate charges or duplicate accidental purchases.
  • You paid successfully but fulfillment failed and the product cannot be delivered.
  • The product cannot be made to run on a supported Windows system after reasonable troubleshooting.
  • A materially incorrect product or bundle was delivered.
  • A refund is otherwise required by applicable law.

4. Change-of-mind requests

Change-of-mind requests after successful digital delivery are reviewed case by case and are not guaranteed. SafiSolutions may consider factors such as whether the file was delivered, whether support was attempted, and whether the request appears to involve abuse or repeated downloading.

5. Bundles

Bundle purchases are treated as one order. Partial refunds for one item inside a delivered bundle are generally not offered unless SafiSolutions determines that a specific fulfillment or technical problem justifies one.

6. Refund processing

Approved refunds are returned through the original Stripe payment method. Bank or card processing times are controlled by the payment provider and may vary.

7. Support first

If the problem is technical, use the Support & Updates page. Many installation, format, printer, codec, or Windows-permission issues can be resolved without losing the purchase.
