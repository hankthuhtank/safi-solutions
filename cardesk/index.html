Where the energy math comes from

Fuel: miles ÷ MPG × price per gallon. Electric: miles ÷ 100 × kWh per 100 miles × price per kWh. Use wall-energy consumption (including charging losses) and your actual blended charging price. Plug-in hybrids need separate fuel and electric mileage budgets.
