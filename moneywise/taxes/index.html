4 things to know

Costly mistakes

  • Celebrating a big refund$126/check

    InsteadUse the IRS Tax Withholding Estimator and update your W-4 withholding.

  • Treating a deduction like a credit$220 vs $1,000

    InsteadA $1,000 deduction saves $220 in the 22% bracket. A $1,000 credit saves the full $1,000.

  • Not saving for tax on side income15.3% + tax

    InsteadSide income owes self-employment tax too. Set aside about a third and pay quarterly.

  • Missing the new deductionsup to $25,000

    InsteadTips, the overtime premium and new-car loan interest come off on Schedule 1-A, even without itemizing.

  • Selling investments in the wrong year0% missed

    InsteadLong-term capital gains are taxed 0% while taxable income stays under $49,450 (single).
