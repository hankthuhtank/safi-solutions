4 things to know

Costly mistakes

  • Paying only the minimum$7,811

    InsteadThat's the interest on $5,000. Pay a fixed amount instead: $192 a month clears it in three years.

  • Carrying a balance to “build credit”$1,118/yr

    InsteadInterest does nothing for your score. Pay the statement balance in full every month.

  • Letting one bill go 30 days late7 years

    InsteadThat is how long it can stay on your report. Put every minimum on autopay, then pay more by hand.

  • Closing your oldest cardscore drop

    InsteadIt shortens your history and raises credit utilization. Keep no-fee cards open with a small bill on autopay.

  • Opening new cards before a big loanhigher rate

    InsteadEach application is a hard inquiry. Wait 6 to 12 months before a mortgage or car loan.
