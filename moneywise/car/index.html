4 things to know

Costly mistakes

  • Shopping by monthly payment+$1,755

    InsteadStretching to 84 months adds that much interest. Agree on the out-the-door price first, in writing.

  • Rolling an old loan into the new one$6,884 avg

    InsteadKeep the current car longer, or pay the gap down in cash before you trade.

  • Taking dealer financing without a quote$1,678/pt

    InsteadGet pre-approved by a bank or credit union first. Each point of markup costs that much here.

  • Financing $2,000 of add-ons$2,410

    InsteadSay no at the table. GAP is often cheaper from your own insurer.

  • Missing the new interest deduction$636/yr

    InsteadCheck the window sticker for U.S. final assembly and claim it on Schedule 1-A.
