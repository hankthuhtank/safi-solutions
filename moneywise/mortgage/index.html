4 things to know

Costly mistakes

  • Budgeting on the loan payment alone$717/mo

    InsteadPlan on the full payment, plus about 1% of the home's value a year for repairs.

  • Waiting for PMI to drop on its own$2,310

    InsteadNote when you'll owe 80% of the original price and ask your servicer, in writing, to cancel PMI.

  • Comparing rates instead of Loan Estimates$1,500+

    InsteadGet three Loan Estimates the same day. Compare fees on page 2 and the APR on page 3.

  • Refinancing into a fresh 30 years$21,297

    InsteadCompare what you will pay in total, not the monthly payment. A shorter new term often wins.

  • Buying points you won't keep$3,696/pt

    InsteadOnly pay for points if you will keep the loan past the break-even, often 5+ years.
