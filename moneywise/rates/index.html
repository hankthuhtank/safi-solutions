4 things to know

Costly mistakes

  • Keeping $10,000 at the average savings rate$368/yr

    InsteadUse an FDIC-insured high-yield savings account or Treasury bills for cash you won't spend soon.

  • Saving at 4% while a card charges 22%$1,099/yr

    InsteadThat is the yearly cost on $6,000. Keep a small emergency fund, then pay the card down.

  • Reading a card's APR as its true cost25.05%

    InsteadInterest is added daily, so a 22.36% APR really costs 25.05% a year on a carried balance.

  • Waiting for a Fed cut to buy a house6.18→6.19%

    InsteadMortgage rates barely moved through 1.75 points of cuts. Watch the 10-year Treasury instead.

  • "No interest if paid in full" promosall back interest

    InsteadMiss by a dollar and deferred interest is charged back to day one. Divide the balance by the promo months and pay that.
