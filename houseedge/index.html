SLOTS / 05

RTP is long-run math. Volatility is the ride.

A 96% RTP does not mean you will get 96% of a session back. It describes the game's modeled long-run return. Volatility tells you whether results tend to arrive in smaller frequent hits or larger, less frequent swings.
