01 / BLACKJACK

Rules that actually matter

3:2 blackjack payout

A $100 natural blackjack wins $150. A 6:5 table only pays $120 and meaningfully increases the house edge.

Dealer stands on soft 17

S17 is generally better for the player than H17.

Double after split

DAS improves the value of splitting pairs, especially aces and eights.

Surrender

When offered, surrender lets you give up half a bet instead of playing a very weak hand to the end.

BEST FIRST HABIT

Use the chart every hand.

Basic strategy removes most of the expensive guesswork. Learn the table first, then worry about speed.

02 / BACCARAT

Most of baccarat is automatic. Your real decision is the bet.

The dealer handles the drawing rules. You choose Banker, Player or Tie. That simple choice creates a huge difference in house edge.

BEST STANDARD BET

Banker

1.06%

Typically the lowest house edge under standard 8-deck commission rules.

SECOND

Player

1.24%

No commission, still a relatively low-edge wager.

LOOKS EXCITING

Tie

14.36%

Large payout, much worse long-run math. Avoid it as a routine bet.

1

Cards are valued 0–9

Tens and face cards count as zero. Only the last digit of the total matters.

2

Player follows a simple draw rule

Player normally draws on 0–5 and stands on 6–7. Naturals 8 or 9 stop the hand.

3

Banker is conditional

Banker's third-card rules depend on Player's result. The dealer handles this automatically.

04 / ROULETTE

The extra zero is the entire story.

Bet type changes variance, but the wheel determines the edge. If you can choose between single-zero and double-zero, the single-zero game is materially better.

EUROPEAN02.70%37 pockets
AMERICAN0 + 005.26%38 pockets
FRENCH02.70%*special even-money rules can reduce effective edge

Outside bets

Red/black, odd/even, high/low. Lower variance, same base wheel edge.

Inside bets

Straights, splits, streets and corners. Bigger payouts, higher variance, same wheel edge.

Past spins

They do not make a number “due.” Each spin is independent.

Best choice

Prefer single-zero. If La Partage or En Prison applies to even-money bets, that can improve the effective edge further.

05 / SLOTS

RTP tells you the long-run average. Volatility tells you how the ride feels.

Two slots can have the same RTP and behave completely differently session to session.

RTP

Return to Player

A long-run modeled percentage across a very large number of spins. A 96% RTP does not promise 96% back in one session.

VOL

Volatility

Low volatility tends toward smaller, more frequent wins. High volatility tends toward larger, less frequent wins.

HIT

Hit frequency

How often a spin produces any win. It does not tell you whether the win is larger than the bet.

FEATURE

Bonus mechanics

Free spins, multipliers, wilds and jackpots change how value is distributed, not whether short-term results are predictable.

LOW VOLATILITYSmaller swings, more frequent hits
HIGH VOLATILITYBigger swings, longer dry spells

Important: volatility changes the distribution of outcomes, not the long-run RTP.

07 / SPORTSBOOK

Bet the number, not the team name.

A sports bet is a price on an outcome. The core skill is learning to read the line and compare that price to your estimate of what is likely to happen.

Why -110 matters

At -110, a $110 risk wins $100. The break-even probability is 52.38%, not 50%. That gap is part of the bookmaker's margin.

THE PART THAT MATTERS MOST

Better strategy can reduce bad decisions. It cannot make gambling predictable.

House edge and vig describe long-run math, not what will happen tonight.

Short-term variance can overwhelm even a small mathematical edge.

Set a budget before playing and treat it as entertainment money.

Never chase losses or increase risk just because you are down.
