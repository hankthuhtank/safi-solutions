Behind the photographs

45 days.
A different perspective.

From shore to crest.

Shorecrest began with 45 days on the road. Mountains, ocean, long drives, and quiet places that made the rest of life feel a little smaller. I came home with photographs, and a feeling I wanted to hold onto: there is more to life than the same routine, over and over.

The name carries that idea. The shore is a place to start. The crest is the top of the wave or ridge, where you can look back and see how far you have come. It is for the people finding their freedom outside, and the ones finding a little peace on the way back up.

I wanted those moments to have a life beyond my camera roll. A canvas on the wall. A mug for slow mornings. A little of the road, carried into the everyday.
