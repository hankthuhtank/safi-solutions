About

Hi, I’m Helal.

I build desktop software, websites and interactive tools that make complicated things easier to work with.

A lot of these projects start with something I wanted to understand better — a sport, a car, a circuit — or a business that needed a better way to run its day. The Bench makes coding approachable. VoltVisual turns electrical systems into something you can explore. SafiStudios brings the same thinking to business software.

I had a basic coding foundation from college, but AI dramatically expanded what I could bring to life. Learning to engineer with it — structuring the problem, guiding the tools and refining the output — is what lets me turn ideas into working products.

I’m based in Paris, Texas. Safi Solutions is my independent practice, so when you get in touch, you’re talking to the person doing the work.
