ABOUT / THE PERSON BEHIND THE WORK01 PERSON
HELAL SAFI
PARIS, TEXAS

Hi, I’m Helal.

I build desktop software, websites and interactive tools that make complicated things easier to work with.

A lot of these projects start with something I want to understand better, or a business that needs a better way to manage its day. The Bench makes coding more approachable. VoltVisual turns electrical systems into something you can explore. SafiStudios brings that same thinking to business software.

I had a basic coding foundation from college, but AI dramatically expanded what I could bring to life. Learning how to engineer with it, structure problems, guide the tools and refine the output is what lets me turn ideas into working products.

I’m based in Paris, Texas. Safi Solutions is my independent practice, so when you get in touch, you’re talking to the person doing the work.

The project list is also a fairly accurate record of my curiosity.
